TERMS & POLICIES

1. HOYSII GUARANTEE
We protect your payments to ensure a safe booking experience.
Secure Holding: Your funds are held securely by Hoysii. The host is not paid until 24 hours after you successfully check in.
Money Back Promise: If the hotel turns you away or the room is significantly different from the photos, you will receive 100% of your money back.

2. BOOKING POLICY
Reservations: A booking is only confirmed once payment is successfully processed.
Check-in Requirements: You must present a valid ID and your booking confirmation at the hotel reception.
Price Changes: Prices are set by the hotel and are final at the time of booking.

3. CANCELLATION POLICY 
Free Cancellation: You may cancel free of charge up to 24 hours before your scheduled check-in time.
Late Cancellation: Cancellations made less than 24 hours before check-in will incur a fee equivalent to the first night's stay and Hoysii service fee.
No-Shows: If you do not arrive on the check-in date, half of the booking cost is non-refundable.

4. REFUND PROCESS
Processing Time: Approved refunds are processed within 5-7 business days.
Method: Refunds are strictly returned to the original payment method (e.g., Waafi, EVC Plus, or Credit Card). Cash refunds are not available.
Disputes: To claim a refund under the "Money Back Promise," you must contact support within 24 hours of your scheduled check-in.

5. PRIVACY STATEMENT
Data Collection: We strictly collect only what is needed: Name, Phone Number, and Booking Details.
Sharing: Your details are shared only with the specific hotel you book to secure your room.
No Spam: We do not sell your data to third parties.

6. CONTACT
For disputes and other questions: call us on WhatsApp by clicking the support button on the profile
