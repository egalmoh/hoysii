# Hoysii Product Brief

## Product
Hoysii is a hotel and accommodation booking platform focused primarily on Somali diaspora travelers returning to Somalia.

The product is not only hotel search. Its promise is a reliable arrival experience:

**Discover → Search → Compare → Book → Confirm → Travel → Airport Pickup → Check-in → Stay → Review**

## Primary customer
A Somali diaspora traveler living abroad who is planning a trip to Somalia and needs trustworthy accommodation.

Initial origin markets:
- UK
- Sweden / Nordics
- Minnesota / US
- Canada

Initial destinations:
- Mogadishu first
- Hargeisa
- Garowe
- Bosaso

## Core problem
Travelers may be unsure whether:
- Hotel photos are current
- A room is truly reserved
- The price will be honored
- The hotel is suitable
- Airport pickup is reliable
- Someone will help if something goes wrong

## Value proposition
**Book your stay back home with confidence.**

Core benefits:
- Verified hotels
- Clear room information and pricing
- Secure booking and payment
- Hotel confirmation
- Optional airport pickup
- Flight-aware arrival coordination
- Customer support
- Verified reviews
- “Need help choosing? Chat with us.”

## Product surfaces
### Public website / SEO
Acquisition, destination pages, hotel pages, travel guides, hotel search.

### Traveler app
Search, compare, book, pay, trip management, airport pickup, support, reviews.

### Hotel partner portal
Reservations, availability, rooms, rates, property details, reviews, payouts.

### Admin / operations
Bookings, arrivals, hotel confirmations, drivers, exceptions, customers, payments, support.

## Distribution
Primary:
1. Google Search + SEO
2. TikTok / Instagram UGC
3. Meta ads
4. Influencers
5. Retargeting
6. Referrals / repeat users

Secondary:
7. Travel agencies

Travel agencies should not be a dependency.

## Traveler app structure
Main navigation:
- Home
- Explore
- Trips
- Saved
- Profile

### Search
Destination, dates, guests.

### Results
Show verification, nightly price, total price, photos, reviews, area, airport distance, breakfast, Wi-Fi, pickup, room availability.

### Help choosing
Place after several results and on hotel detail pages:
> **Need help choosing? Chat with us.**

### Hotel page
Photos, verified badge, rooms, prices, amenities, map, cancellation, reviews, pickup.

### Checkout
1. Guest information
2. Extras
3. Payment
4. Confirmation

### Trip page
Show:
- Flight
- Airport pickup
- Hotel
- Driver name
- Vehicle
- License plate
- Booking status
- Check-in
- Support
