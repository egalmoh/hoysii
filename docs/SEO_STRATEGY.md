# Hoysii SEO & Public Website Strategy

## Goal
The public website should:
1. Acquire high-intent travelers from Google
2. Move them directly into booking

SEO and booking should feel like one product.

## Search intent priority
High intent first:
- hotels in Mogadishu
- Mogadishu hotel
- hotels near Mogadishu airport
- best hotels in Mogadishu
- family hotels Mogadishu
- luxury hotels Mogadishu
- cheap hotels Mogadishu

Then informational:
- where to stay in Mogadishu
- visiting Mogadishu
- Somalia summer travel
- airport to hotel

## SEO principles
- Avoid thin duplicate pages
- Publish only where real inventory exists
- Use unique hotel content
- Keep hotel information current
- Add structured data where appropriate
- Prioritize speed and mobile
- Show prices clearly
- Link guides to relevant booking pages
- Link hotel pages to city/category pages

## Conversion
Every SEO page should offer:
- Search
- Check availability
- View rooms
- Compare hotels
- Need help choosing? Chat with us
