# Hoysii Customer Journey

## Short version
**Discover → Search → Compare → Book → Confirm → Add Flight → Prepare → Arrive → Transfer → Check-in → Stay → Review & Return**

## 1. Discover
Customer sees Hoysii through TikTok, Instagram, Google, Meta ads, influencers, referral, or an agency.

Hoysii tracks source, creator/referral codes, and retargeting audiences.

## 2. Search
Customer enters:
- Destination
- Check-in
- Check-out
- Guests

Hoysii returns relevant, bookable, verified inventory.

## 3. Compare
Customer compares:
- Photos
- Price
- Reviews
- Location
- Airport distance
- Breakfast
- Wi-Fi
- Cancellation
- Airport pickup

If unsure:
> **Need help choosing? Chat with us.**

Support can recommend 2–3 options based on budget and needs.

## 4. Book
Customer selects room and optional pickup, then pays.

Hoysii:
- Holds inventory during checkout
- Creates booking
- Generates booking ID
- Notifies hotel
- Sends confirmation

## 5. Confirm
Customer receives:
- Booking ID
- Hotel
- Room
- Dates
- Total paid
- Cancellation rules
- Support contact

Hotel acknowledges the reservation.

Recommended lifecycle:
**Pending → Paid → Hotel Confirmed → Arrival Scheduled → Checked In → Completed**

## 6. Add Flight
Customer provides:
- Flight number
- Travel date

Hoysii looks up:
- Airline
- Route
- Scheduled arrival
- Estimated arrival
- Actual arrival
- Status

Customer confirms the matched flight.

## 7. Prepare
Before departure Hoysii:
- Reconfirms hotel
- Rechecks flight
- Assigns driver
- Confirms pickup
- Flags missing info

## 8. Arrive
Traveler lands.

Hoysii knows:
- Customer
- Flight
- Arrival time
- Hotel
- Driver
- Pickup status

## 9. Transfer
Driver meets traveler and takes them to the hotel.

Statuses:
- Driver assigned
- Accepted
- At airport
- Passenger found
- Picked up
- At hotel
- Completed

## 10. Check-in
Hotel already has the reservation and payment status.

Customer should not need to renegotiate price or explain the booking.

If the hotel fails, Hoysii owns the recovery.

## 11. Stay
Customer can:
- Contact support
- Extend stay
- Report an issue

Support already sees booking, hotel, flight, driver, payment, and history.

## 12. Post-stay
Customer:
- Leaves verified review
- Refers friends
- Rebooks later

Hoysii records hotel quality, referrals, and repeat behavior.
